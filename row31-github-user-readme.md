# Ashtar Trade Test & Technical Training Centre — Multan, Pakistan

Technical training institute in Multan, Pakistan, founded in 2021 by Shahid Abbas. Practical training in welding, electrical work, pipe fitting and fabrication, mechanical fitting, HSE and civil surveying — with trade-test preparation and Job Placement Assistance for Gulf jobs in Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Qatar and Oman.

## What we teach

- **6G Welding** — SMAW, TIG and MIG welding, including the 6G fixed-position pipe test. https://www.ashtartradetest.com/courses/welding-training
- **Electrician / Industrial Electrician** — wiring, installation and industrial electrical work.
- **Pipe Fitter and Pipe Fabricator** — pipework fitting and fabrication to drawing and specification. https://www.ashtartradetest.com/courses/pipe-fitter — https://www.ashtartradetest.com/courses/pipe-fabricator
- **Mechanical Fitter** — fitting, assembly and maintenance of mechanical equipment. https://www.ashtartradetest.com/courses/mechanical-fitter
- **Civil Surveyor** — surveying skills for construction work.
- **Safety Officer (HSE)** — health, safety and environment practice for industrial sites.
- **Plumber, Steel Fixer, Shuttering Carpenter, Mason** — core construction trades.

## Trade-test preparation

A trade test is a hands-on exam set by Gulf employers or manpower agencies: the worker performs a real task and the result decides the job offer. Beginners build up to test standard step by step; experienced workers come to sharpen a specific skill, practise the test task, and walk in ready.
